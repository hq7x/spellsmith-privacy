Privacy Policy — Spell-Smith: Weapon Swarm

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This policy explains what information Spell-Smith: Weapon Swarm ("the game"), published by Spell-Smith Games ("we"), handles and why.

Information we do not collect

The game has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Game data stored on your device

Your progress (unlocked stages, currencies, upgrades, skins and settings) is saved only in the game's local storage on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. To deliver an item, the game receives a purchase token and order ID from Google Play and stores them on your device so the same purchase is not delivered twice. Google's handling of payment data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchase verification. To stop fraud and to handle refunds, each purchase is checked with Google Play by our server (hosted on Google Firebase / Google Cloud) before the item is delivered. For this the game sends our server the purchase token, the product ID and a random identifier created for this installation of the game. We store a one-way hash of the purchase token, the order ID, the product ID, that random installation identifier, and whether the purchase was granted or refunded. This record is not linked to your name, email address or Google account on our side. We keep it for as long as needed for accounting and fraud prevention, and no longer than 5 years and use it only to verify purchases and reverse refunded ones.

Similarly, when you watch an optional rewarded ad for a free chest, Google AdMob confirms the completed view to our server together with the same random installation identifier and a one-time code, so each reward can be claimed only once. We store that confirmation and whether it was claimed.

Mystery boxes award random cosmetic Skin Shards (combined to craft cosmetic skins) and, in some boxes, Perk Rune cards that upgrade in-game abilities. The odds for every box and every item are shown in the in-game store ("Drop rates") before purchase.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate an approximate location), device and app information, and interactions with ads. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings.

Players who buy the Ad-Free Pass do not see interstitial ads; optional "watch an ad for a reward" offers remain available and are never required.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

The only data we hold is the purchase-verification record described above. To access or delete yours, contact us with the order ID from your Google Play receipt. For data processed by Google (advertising and payments), use your Google Account controls or contact Google.

Changes

We will update this page if the game's data handling changes, and change the date above.
